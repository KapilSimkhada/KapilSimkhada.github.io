RESEARCH

Research Interests

My research integrates plant pathology with genetics, genomics and molecular breeding to understand and improve host resistance.

🧬

Plant Genetics & Genomics

Understanding genetic variation associated with disease resistance in soybean, dry bean and other crop species.

🌱

Host Resistance

Characterizing resistant germplasm and investigating genetic mechanisms controlling plant resistance to pathogens and nematodes.

🧪

Molecular Breeding

Applying molecular markers, KASP assays, resistance loci and genotype–phenotype relationships to crop improvement.

💻

Computational Genomics

Using comparative genomics, pangenomics, R, Linux and high-performance computing to investigate biological variation.
