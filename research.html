RESEARCH

Research

My research integrates plant pathology, genetics, molecular biology and computational genomics to understand genetic resistance in crop plants.

Soybean Genetics & Host Resistance

My soybean research focuses on characterizing genetic resistance to soybean cyst nematode across breeding lines and soybean germplasm.

I use controlled-environment phenotyping to evaluate resistance to different soybean cyst nematode HG types and investigate relationships between resistance phenotypes and major resistance loci.

Research approaches

  • Phenotypic screening of soybean breeding lines and germplasm
  • Soybean cyst nematode resistance evaluation
  • Female index calculation and resistance classification
  • Evaluation across different HG types
  • Genotype–phenotype analysis

Rhg1 and Rhg4 Resistance Genetics

A major component of my molecular research focuses on the genetic architecture of soybean resistance associated with the Rhg1 and Rhg4 loci.

My work includes molecular marker analysis, KASP genotyping and investigation of genetic variation associated with resistance.

Methods and concepts

  • KASP genotyping
  • Allelic discrimination
  • Rhg4 / GmSHMT08 marker analysis
  • Rhg1 copy-number variation
  • qPCR
  • Marker validation

Dry Bean Resistance Genetics

My dry bean research evaluates genetic variation in resistance to soybean cyst nematode across breeding lines and commercial bean classes.

This work integrates field experiments with controlled-environment resistance assays, allowing resistance phenotypes observed under different environments to be compared.

Research components

  • Field resistance screening
  • Controlled-environment phenotyping
  • Reproduction factor analysis
  • Female index analysis
  • Correlation of field and controlled-environment responses

Computational Genomics

I am also developing computational expertise in comparative genomics and bioinformatics.

My microbial genomics coursework included comparative pangenome analysis of complete Xanthomonas campestris genomes using high-performance computing workflows.

Computational approaches

  • NCBI genomic data retrieval
  • Genome annotation
  • Pangenome analysis
  • Core and accessory genome analysis
  • Linux and Bash workflows
  • High-performance computing
  • R-based data analysis
